MACD 12 / 26 / 9

MACD compares two exponential moving averages to study momentum.

Settings

  • Fast EMA: 12
  • Slow EMA: 26
  • Signal: 9

Main Line

The MACD line is calculated from the 12 EMA minus the 26 EMA.

Signal Line

The signal line is a 9-period EMA of the MACD line.

Basic Interpretation

  • MACD above signal → positive momentum context
  • MACD below signal → negative momentum context
