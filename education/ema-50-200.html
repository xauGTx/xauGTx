EMA 50 & EMA 200

EMA is a moving average that gives more weight to recent prices.

EMA 200

EMA 200 is commonly used to identify the broader market trend.

EMA 50

EMA 50 can help identify the medium-term direction.

Simple Rule

  • Price above EMA 200 → bullish context
  • Price below EMA 200 → bearish context
  • EMA 50 above EMA 200 → bullish structure
  • EMA 50 below EMA 200 → bearish structure

Use EMA with structure, momentum and risk management instead of using it alone.
