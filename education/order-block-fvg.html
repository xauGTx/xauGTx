Order Block & FVG

Order Block

In SMC terminology, an order block is commonly identified around a significant candle or price area preceding an impulsive move.

Fair Value Gap

FVG describes an imbalance between candles where price moved rapidly and left a gap-like inefficiency in the three-candle structure.

Workflow

  • Identify market structure
  • Find BOS or possible MSS
  • Watch liquidity
  • Locate potential OB
  • Locate potential FVG
  • Wait for confirmation
  • Define SL and TP before entry
