XAUUSD Market Structure

Bullish Structure

A sequence of Higher Highs and Higher Lows can describe bullish structure.

Bearish Structure

A sequence of Lower Highs and Lower Lows can describe bearish structure.

BOS

A Break of Structure occurs when price breaks a relevant previous swing level.

CHOCH / MSS

A possible market-structure shift occurs when price breaks an important level against the previous directional structure.

SMC Workflow

Structure → BOS → MSS → Liquidity → Order Block → FVG → Confirmation → Risk.
