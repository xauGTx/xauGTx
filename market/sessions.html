XAUUSD Trading Sessions

Market activity can change during different global trading sessions. Times depend on daylight-saving rules and the timezone being used.

Asian Session

Often associated with the early part of the global trading day.

London Session

European market activity can increase liquidity and volatility.

New York Session

US market activity can produce significant movement in gold, especially around important economic releases.

Risk Reminder

Always verify the current session time and economic calendar before trading.
