XAUUSD EDUCATION

Learn Gold Trading

Understand the basic tools before taking risk.

📈

EMA 200

EMA 200 is commonly used to understand the longer-term market direction.

  • Price above EMA 200 → bullish context
  • Price below EMA 200 → bearish context
  • Use with structure and confirmation
📊

EMA 50

EMA 50 can help identify medium-term momentum and dynamic areas of interest.

  • EMA 50 above EMA 200
  • EMA 50 below EMA 200
  • Look for confirmation
🔥

RSI 9

RSI measures momentum on a scale from 0 to 100.

  • Above 70 can indicate strong momentum
  • Below 30 can indicate strong downside momentum
  • Do not use RSI alone
〽️

MACD

MACD compares moving averages to study momentum and trend changes.

  • MACD line
  • Signal line
  • Histogram
🏗️

Market Structure

Structure helps traders organize price movement using swing highs and lows.

  • HH
  • HL
  • LH
  • LL
💧

Liquidity

Liquidity areas can exist around previous highs, lows and obvious price levels.

  • Liquidity sweep
  • False breakout
  • Confirmation required
🚀

BOS

Break of Structure can indicate that price has broken a meaningful swing level.

  • Bullish BOS
  • Bearish BOS
  • Combine with context
🔄

CHOCH / MSS

CHOCH or MSS can describe a potential change in short-term market structure.

  • Observe previous structure
  • Wait for confirmation
  • Avoid entering from one signal only
📦

Order Block

Order blocks are commonly used by SMC traders to identify areas where price may react.

  • Identify strong displacement
  • Locate relevant candle area
  • Wait for confirmation
🧱

Fair Value Gap

FVG refers to an imbalance between candles that traders may monitor for future price interaction.

  • Bullish FVG
  • Bearish FVG
  • Use with market structure
🎯

Risk / Reward

Risk/reward compares the potential loss with the potential profit of a setup.

  • Define SL first
  • Define TP
  • Calculate ratio
🛡️

Risk Management

Risk management determines how much capital can be exposed to a single trade.

  • Risk per trade
  • Stop loss
  • Position sizing
RISK MANAGEMENT

Protect Your Trading Account

1️⃣ Define Risk

Choose the maximum amount you are willing to lose before entering a trade.

2️⃣ Define Stop Loss

Place your stop based on the trading setup rather than choosing it randomly.

3️⃣ Calculate Position

Position size should be based on your risk amount and stop distance.

4️⃣ Follow Your Plan

Avoid changing your risk because of FOMO, revenge trading or emotional decisions.
