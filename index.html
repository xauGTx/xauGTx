XAUUSD Trading Guide

Simple concepts for traders learning gold markets.

01

EMA 200

EMA 200 can be used as a longer-term trend reference. It should not be treated as a standalone entry signal.

02

EMA 50

EMA 50 can help traders observe medium-term price direction and possible dynamic support or resistance.

03

RSI

RSI measures recent price momentum. Common reference levels include 30 and 70, but context remains important.

04

MACD

MACD compares moving averages to study momentum and possible trend changes.

05

Support & Resistance

Previous highs, lows and important price areas can help identify locations where price has reacted.

06

Risk Management

Position sizing and stop-loss planning are important parts of a trading plan. Never risk money you cannot afford to lose.
